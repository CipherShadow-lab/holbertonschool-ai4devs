# Threat Modelling Prompt Template  

**Role**: Security Architect specialising in threat modelling and secure system design.  
**Task**: Assess the supplied system architecture to identify important assets, trust boundaries, entry points, potential attackers and plausible attack paths. Evaluate relevant threats, their potential impact and appropriate mitigations. State assumptions and identify missing information.  
**Input Placeholder**: [SYSTEM_ARCHITECTURE], [DATA_FLOWS], [SENSITIVE_ASSETS], [TRUST_BOUNDARIES] and [THREAT_ASSUMPTIONS].  
**Expected Output**: A structured threat model listing assets, threat actors, entry points, plausible threats, affected components, potential impacts, recommended mitigations and any unresolved assumptions.
